Your voice stays on your PC

Listening and understanding run on your computer. Here is exactly what that means.

  • Audio is never uploaded or saved. Speech recognition runs locally.
  • No accounts or telemetry. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • Open source. Read every line on GitHub.
  • Online voices are optional. They send only the sentence Jarvis is about to say to Microsoft's read-aloud service. Pick an offline voice to send nothing.
  • It asks first. Messages, shutdown and risky clicks need a spoken "yes".
  • You control the terminal. Only commands on your own list can run.
  • Programs never launch from a search. They're shown in their folder instead.
  • "Jarvis stop" always works, even while it's talking.
